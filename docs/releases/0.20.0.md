ManiOS 0.20.0 brings **drivers**: floppies, CD-ROMs, SATA disks, virtio
disks and network cards, a RealTek 8139 and a DEC Tulip, the PC
speaker, the Sound Blaster 16, AC'97 and the AdLib's FM synthesizer,
serial ports COM2-COM4, the parallel port, the CMOS memory, and ACPI to
turn the machine off. ManiOS is an independent operating system for
i386 PCs with its own kernel (ZKT), userland, desktop (ManiDE) and disk
operating system (ManiDOS); see
[0.19.0](https://github.com/zygvlogs/manios/releases/tag/v0.19.0).

Every driver is ManiOS's own code, BSD-licensed, written from the
hardware's public documentation, and tested on QEMU's models of the
hardware.

This is a **pre-release**, tested in QEMU.

## What's new

- **Disks**: floppy drives (`fd0`; 360 KB to 2.88 MB disks, the format
  found by itself), CD and DVD drives on IDE or SATA (`cd0`), SATA
  disks on AHCI controllers (`sata0`), virtio disks (`vd0`).
- **CDs**: ISO 9660 with Rock Ridge and Joliet long names, at `/n/cd0`,
  which follows the disc in the drive -- take one out, put another in.
  An `.iso` written to a disk or USB stick is read too.
- **Network cards**: RealTek RTL8139 (`rl0`), virtio-net (`vio0`), DEC
  21143 "Tulip" (`dc0`) -- with the NE2000, PCnet and PRO/1000, seven.
- **Sound**: `beep` on the PC speaker; `play FILE.WAV` and `play -t
  440 500` on a Sound Blaster 16 or AC'97 (`/dev/audio`); `fm`, notes
  on an AdLib's (or Sound Blaster's) OPL2 FM synthesizer (`/dev/opl`).
- **Ports and power**: COM2-COM4 (`/dev/com2`, speed in `/dev/com2ctl`),
  the printer port (`/dev/lpt1`), the CMOS memory (`/dev/nvram`),
  `poweroff` and `reboot` (ACPI, `/dev/power`).
- **ManiDOS**: the floppy is B:, SATA and virtio disks get letters after
  the IDE ones, then the CD drives; `VOL` shows a disc's name.
- **`install`** puts ManiOS on SATA and virtio disks too (`install sata0`).

```
cd0: QEMU DVD-ROM, ATAPI, disc of 2 MiB

```

Guide: [docs/drivers.md](https://github.com/zygvlogs/manios/blob/v0.20.0/docs/drivers.md).

## Changed

- QEMU's PC has a CD drive, a floppy drive, a PC speaker, a parallel port
  and ACPI, so `ls /dev` lists `cd0`, `fd0`, `beep`, `lpt1`, `nvram`
  and `power` there, and `/n/cd0` exists (empty without a disc).
- ManiDOS starts on the first hard disk (not the first drive after B:),
  or A: without one.

## Known limits

No USB, Intel HD Audio, NVMe or SCSI yet. CDs are read only; a floppy
is mounted if a disk is in at boot. One sound format for `/dev/audio`
(16-bit stereo, 44.1 kHz), no volume control. ACPI turns the machine
off and resets it, nothing more. None of the drivers has met real
hardware yet: reports are welcome. Otherwise as 0.19.0: ManiOS's FAT
drives are read-only to files; no floating point in programs; no
signals.

licenses (see `NOTICES.txt`).
